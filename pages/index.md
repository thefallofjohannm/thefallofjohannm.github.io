# Home

It took some time but in the end 
I came to a decision. I shall manage this blog in 
the worst way possible.
That is, I will mix all the languages.
English is going to be the backbone of this blog.
That's why there's __Home__ and __Music__ instead of 
__Domů__ or __Domov__ or 
__Domovská stránka__ and __Hudba__ or __Muzika__ and so on. 
However,
some posts will be in English only, some will be strictly in Czech.
If necessary, I will use Spanish, I'm not afraid! 
And instead of coupling the pages, 
I will just create two individual posts of the same content 
but in a different language.

If you look to the left, you will see the options. If you look to the right, you will see your reflection.
